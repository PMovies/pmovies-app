Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: PMovies is a film community. You can browse it without an account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo so you can have a profile and post. What you post (reviews, comments, lists, theater visits) is public. We do not sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Who we are

PMovies ("we", "us") runs the website at p-movies.com. For anything about this policy or your data, write to p.movies155@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google

Sign-in is handled by Google through Firebase Authentication. We ask only for basic profile access and receive:

  • your name, email address and profile photo;
  • a unique account ID that Google generates for PMovies.

We never see or store your Google password, and we do not access your contacts, Gmail, Drive or any other Google data. Your email address is kept in the sign-in system and is not shown on your public profile.

What you add yourself

  • Profile: nickname, username, bio, photo and backdrop, favourite films and people.
  • Content: reviews, ratings, comments, Lobby posts and replies, lists, fan art you upload, and articles you submit to The Journal.
  • Activity: likes, follows, votes in the Top 100 and polls, Film of the Day ratings, your watchlist, and theater visits you log for the Theater Race.

Collected automatically

  • Voting integrity: to keep votes fair we store a scrambled (hashed) identifier of your browser and, if you vote by email, a hashed version of that email together with basic timing signals from the voting session. The hashes cannot be turned back into your email or device.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, we store the subscription address your browser gives us so we can send them.
  • Usage statistics: pages visited, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, collected through Google Analytics and Cloudflare.
  • On your device: the site saves preferences, your sign-in session, recently viewed films and similar items in your browser's local storage so it works smoothly between visits.

The image search feature ("PMovies Lens") analyses your picture inside your own browser. The image is not uploaded to us.

How we use it

  • to create and show your profile and the content you post;
  • to run community features: likes, follows, notifications, leaderboards and voting;
  • to prevent duplicate votes, spam and abuse;
  • to understand how the site is used and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your Google data for advertising.

What is public

Your nickname, username, photo, bio, reviews, comments, lists, fan art, follower counts and Theater Race entries are visible to anyone. Lobby discussions are visible to signed-in members. Your email address, notifications, watchlist sync data and individual vote records are not public.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile and sign-in record and delete or anonymise your content within 30 days. Aggregated vote totals that no longer identify you may remain.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete content: you can edit your profile and edit or delete your own reviews and lists from the site.
  • Delete your account or get a copy of your data: email p.movies155@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.
  • Remove Google access: you can disconnect PMovies at any time from your Google Account connections.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your browser or device settings.
  • Local data: clear your browser's site data for p-movies.com.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain uses of your data, or to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

PMovies is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes we will also post a notice on the site.
